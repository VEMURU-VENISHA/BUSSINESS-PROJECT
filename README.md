# 🥤 Optimizing Beverage Sales: Placing the Right Beverage in the Right Market

A Business Data Management (BDM) capstone project focused on analyzing beverage sales data across multiple US regions to identify sales imbalance, profitability gaps, and regional demand differences. The project uses Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), statistical analysis, correlation analysis, and visualizations to uncover business insights and recommend region-specific pricing, inventory, and marketing strategies. The analysis was performed on 9,647+ sales records containing retailer, regional, product, pricing, and profitability data.

## 🚀 Key Highlights
- Regional sales and profitability analysis
- Retailer and beverage performance comparison
- Correlation and statistical analysis
- Time-series sales trend analysis
- Data-driven business recommendations
- Inventory and pricing optimization insights

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
Python • Pandas • NumPy • Matplotlib • Seaborn • Excel • Statistical Analysis • EDA

## 📈 Outcome
Identified top-performing beverages, underperforming regions, pricing inefficiencies, and operational trends to support better business decisions, reduce wastage, and improve profitability.
